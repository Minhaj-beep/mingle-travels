About us

Born in Sivasagar, made for the road

Our story

Thirteen years ago, we set out to show people the Northeast the way locals know it.

The North Eastern part of India is widely regarded as "Paradise Unexplored". Its eight states hold a wealth of natural beauty, wildlife, culture and history, from the Ahom capital on our doorstep to Himalayan monasteries and the wettest hills on earth.

Mingle Travels began in Amtal Chariali, Sivasagar, to help travellers experience all of it without the stress. Today we plan everything from a single car hire to multi-state family holidays and group departures to Nepal, handling the routes, vehicles, drivers and stays so you can simply enjoy the journey.

The eight sisters

One region, eight worlds

Each Northeast state has its own landscapes, languages and food. We'll help you find your favourite.

01

Assam

Tea gardens, Kaziranga's rhinos, Majuli and the Ahom heritage of Sivasagar.

02

Arunachal Pradesh

Tawang's monastery, Sela Pass, Ziro Valley and the Golden Pagoda.

03

Meghalaya

Living root bridges, waterfalls, caves and crystal-clear rivers.

04

Nagaland

Warrior heritage, Kohima and the famous Hornbill Festival.

05

Manipur

Loktak Lake's floating islands and classical Manipuri dance.

06

Mizoram

Rolling blue hills, bamboo forests and the calm of Aizawl.

07

Tripura

Royal palaces, ancient rock carvings and the Neermahal water palace.

08

Sikkim

Kanchenjunga views, high lakes and Buddhist monasteries.
