1 day
Assam · Sivasagar

Ahom Heritage Trail

For six centuries Sivasagar was the heart of the Ahom kingdom. Spend a day among its palaces, temples and royal burial mounds, all within easy reach of our office.

  • Rang Ghar
  • Talatal Ghar
  • Shivadol
  • Charaideo Moidams
Day-by-day plan
  1. Morning: Shivadol and the Sivasagar tank, then Rang Ghar, Asia's oldest surviving amphitheatre.
  2. Afternoon: The palace complex of Talatal Ghar and Kareng Ghar.
  3. Evening: Charaideo Moidams, the UNESCO-listed royal mounds, before returning to Sivasagar.

Assam · Kaziranga National Park

Kaziranga Wildlife Escape

A UNESCO World Heritage park of tall elephant grass and wetlands, home to the greater one-horned rhino, wild elephants, water buffalo and tigers.

  • Jeep safari
  • Elephant safari
  • Tea estates
  • Birdwatching
Day-by-day plan
  1. Day 1: Drive from Sivasagar or Jorhat to Kaziranga. Evening at the orchid park and tea gardens.
  2. Day 2: Early-morning elephant safari, afternoon jeep safari in the Central or Western range.
  3. Day 3: Optional sunrise safari in the Eastern range, then drop to Jorhat airport or home.

Assam · Majuli

Majuli River Island

Cross the Brahmaputra by ferry to the world's largest river island, the cradle of Assam's Vaishnavite culture, with satras, mask-making and village life.

  • Ferry crossing
  • Samaguri Satra masks
  • Mising villages
  • River sunsets
Day-by-day plan
  1. Day 1: Drive to Nimati Ghat and take the ferry to Majuli. Evening sunset by the river.
  2. Day 2: Visit Auniati, Kamalabari and Samaguri satras, and watch traditional masks being made.
  3. Day 3: Morning in a Mising village, then ferry back and drive home.

Arunachal Pradesh · Tawang

Tawang & Sela Pass

A classic Himalayan road trip over the 13,700 ft Sela Pass to Tawang, home to India's largest monastery and some of the most dramatic views in the country.

  • Sela Pass
  • Tawang Monastery
  • Dirang
  • Mountain lakes
Day-by-day plan
  1. Day 1: Drive to Bhalukpong via Tezpur.
  2. Day 2: Climb through Bomdila to the apple orchards of Dirang.
  3. Day 3: Cross Sela Pass and its frozen lake into Tawang.
  4. Day 4: Tawang Monastery, War Memorial and local markets.
  5. Day 5: Day trip to the high lakes (permit and weather permitting), then back down to Dirang.
  6. Day 6: Return drive to Assam.

Abode of Clouds
