About us

Local roots, thirteen years of happy travellers

The North Eastern part of India is widely called "Paradise Unexplored". Its eight states hold a wealth of natural beauty, wildlife, culture and history, and much of it is still off the usual tourist trail.

Mingle Travels started in Sivasagar to help people see it properly. From a single car hire to a multi-state family holiday, we handle the planning, vehicles and stays so you can enjoy the journey.
