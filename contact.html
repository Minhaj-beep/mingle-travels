Good to know

Frequently asked

Can you customise a tour for my family?

Yes. Every itinerary on our site is a starting point. Tell us your dates, budget and interests and we'll build a trip around you.

Do I need a permit for Arunachal Pradesh?

Indian visitors need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) for most of Arunachal Pradesh, and foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit. We'll guide you through the process when you book.

Do your cars come with a driver?

Yes. All our vehicles come with experienced local drivers who know the roads, the weather and the best stops along the way.

When is the best time to visit the Northeast?

October to April is ideal for most places. Kaziranga is usually open from November to April, while Meghalaya's waterfalls are at their best during and just after the monsoon.

Can you pick us up from the airport late at night?

Absolutely. We're open 24 hours, and we pick up from Jorhat, Dibrugarh and Guwahati airports at any time.
